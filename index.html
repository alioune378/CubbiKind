Questions

Does the voice come out of Tukki?

No. The voice plays from the phone you touch to Tukki's heart, so Tukki stays soft and simple, with no speaker, battery or microphone inside.

What phones work?

Most recent iPhones and Android phones can read the tag in the heart. Every Tukki will also have a printed code on its label for older phones.

Does grandma need an app?

No. She touches her phone to her Tukki and her family page opens in the browser. Invites and new messages can arrive by WhatsApp or text.

How does the $5 reservation work?

It saves your place and your $49 early-bird price. When our crowdfunding campaign opens, reservers get the link first. Once you order, we refund your $5. Changed your mind? Reply to any of our emails and we refund it right away.

When will Tukki ship?

We're making the first samples and testing with our first families. Reservers will get the date first, before any order is taken.

Are these photos of the real toys?

Not yet. The photographs are concept visualizations of the planned design, and the drawings are illustrations. Physical samples are in development, and we'll share real photos before the campaign opens.

Is Tukki Hug working today?

Tukki Hug on this site is a demonstration. The real feature will arrive with the Tukkibear app, and we'll share it with our first families before launch.

What happens to our recordings if Tukkibear closes?

They stay yours. You can download every recording at any time, and if we ever shut down, every family will get advance notice and a simple way to keep them.
