How it works

Give one twin away

Keep one bear at home and give or post the other to a grandparent, a parent away from home, or anyone your child misses.

They record their voice

They tap their phone on their twin's heart and record a story, a song or a prayer, in any language. No app to install.

Bedtime, together

At bedtime, tap the heart of your child's bear and their loved one's voice plays while they hug it. Record a reply to send back.

Twin Hug: it only works when both bears are touched

  1. Your child taps their bear. Grandma's phone says: "Awa is hugging her bear right now."
  2. If grandma taps her twin within a few minutes, the hug connects on both phones, and it's saved to your family's Hug Calendar.
  3. Asleep on the other side of the world? The hug waits for her. When she hugs back, your child wakes up to "Grandma hugged you back."

Questions

Does the voice come out of the bear?

The voice plays from the phone you tap on the bear's heart, so the bear stays soft, washable and safe from birth.

What phones work?

Most iPhones and Android phones from recent years can read the tag in the heart. Every bear also has a printed code on its label for older phones.

Does grandma need an app?

No. She taps her bear with her phone and her family page opens in the browser. Messages and invites can come by WhatsApp or text.

When will the bears ship?

We're finishing samples and testing with our first families. Reservers will get the date first, before we take any full payment.
